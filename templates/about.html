System Documentation & Methodology

About SafePath AI & Our Explainable Safety Model

SafePath AI is designed to help pedestrians, cyclists, and commuters make informed navigation choices by blending OpenStreetMap road networks with community-reported safety hazards.

Core Mission & Ethics

Our core objective is to empower citizens with actionable, transparent environmental safety information. We believe personal safety technology must be:

Explainable Every deduction on a route shows exact distances, report severities, and timestamps. No uninterpretable neural black boxes.
Privacy-First Uploaded photos have EXIF GPS tags stripped. Emergency share tokens are hashed, short-lived, and revocable at any moment.
Accountable Clear distinction between verified consensus and unverified pending reports. Moderation audit logs track all actions.

Explainable Route-Risk Scoring Formula

Routes are evaluated along a 250-meter corridor buffer using the following documented weighted mathematical model:

SafetyScore = max(5.0, min(100.0, 100 - ∑ [ BasePenalty × SeverityMult × ProximityMult × RecencyMult × VerificationMult ]))
Model Weight Parameters:
Factor Condition / Category Weight / Multiplier Rationale
1. Hazard Category (Base Penalty) Harassment or Threatening Behavior 24 pts Highest direct personal vulnerability risk.
Suspicious Activity 20 pts Immediate environmental concern requiring avoidance.
Isolated or Poorly Monitored Areas 16 pts Lack of natural surveillance and escape routes.
Blocked Roads or Construction 14 pts Forces pedestrians into vehicular traffic.
Broken Streetlights 12 pts Reduces nighttime visibility and situational awareness.
Road Damage & Potholes 10 pts Physical trip or collision hazard for walkers & cyclists.
Other Safety Concerns 8 pts General environmental alerts.
2. Severity Multiplier High Hazard 1.6× Urgent severity multiplier.
Medium Risk 1.0× Standard baseline multiplier.
Low Concern 0.6× Minor nuisance or low impact.
3. Proximity Decay < 50 meters (Direct) 1.0× Directly on or immediately adjacent to sidewalk.
50 – 150 meters (Nearby) 0.65× Within line of sight or connecting corner.
150 – 250 meters (Perimeter) 0.35× Corridor edge; partial deterrence.
4. Recency Decay ≤ 3 days 1.0× Fresh incident requiring immediate notice.
4 – 14 days 0.8× Recent hazard, possibly still persistent.
15 – 30 days 0.6× Gradual decay as repairs or patrols may have occurred.
> 30 days 0.4× Historical pattern weight.
5. Verification Moderator Verified / 3+ Confirmations 1.0× – 1.25× High confidence level.
Community Pending 0.55× Prevents unverified single reports from distorting score.
Score Categories:
Lower Reported Risk (80–100) Minimal or low-severity hazards along the transit corridor.
Moderate Reported Risk (50–79) Caution advised; isolated reports or broken infrastructure noted.
Higher Reported Risk (0–49) Multiple active high-severity hazard reports along the route.

Data Limitations & Important Disclaimers

Non-Guarantee Notice: The safety score is an explainable heuristic derived strictly from community-reported data and nearby infrastructure reports. It does not constitute a guarantee of personal safety or an endorsement of any path as risk-free.
  • No Presumed Safety in Unmapped Areas: If no community reports exist for a given region, the system flags the corridor as "Insufficient Safety Data" rather than falsely presenting 100/100 safety.
  • No Crime Demographic Profiling: SafePath AI strictly evaluates specific, reportable environmental hazards (lighting, infrastructure damage, road obstructions, harassment reports). It does not infer neighborhood crime rates or demographic attributes without verified factual data.
  • Not a Substitute for 911 / 112: This application is a route discovery and peer assistance tool. In an urgent emergency, always contact emergency dispatch services immediately.

Community Moderation & Privacy Rules

To maintain trust, prevent harassment, and eliminate malicious spam:

  • Photo Privacy: All user-uploaded images are processed server-side to strip embedded camera hardware serials, location tags, and EXIF GPS metadata.
  • Reporter Privacy: Public hazard map popups never disclose the identity, username, or profile of the submitting user.
  • Audit Logging: All moderator approvals, rejections, or severity adjustments are permanently recorded in an administrative audit log.
  • Revocable Location Sharing: Emergency SOS links use cryptographically random 64-character tokens, stored only as SHA-256 hashes, with strict expiry and instant user revocation.
