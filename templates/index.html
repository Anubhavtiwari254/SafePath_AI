Explainable AI

No Black Boxes. Full Transparency.

Unlike opaque systems, SafePath AI explains exactly how every score was calculated so you can make informed decisions:

1
Route Corridor Analysis

Computes the exact shortest Haversine distance from every verified report to the route path within a 250m buffer.

2
Severity & Recency Weighting

High-severity concerns on the sidewalk carry greater weight than historical reports from a month ago.

3
Verified Community Consensus

Reports corroborated by multiple community confirmations or reviewed by moderators carry higher weight.
